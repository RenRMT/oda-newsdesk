Inclusion criteria

Sources that are on topic by nature are included in full. For every other source, an item needs at least one keyword match:

  • Aid & humanitarian: an aid keyword.
  • Aid × data × AI: a data or AI keyword together with an aid signal, or any item from a data or AI source.
  • Netherlands: a Dutch signal (a keyword, Dutch language or a Dutch source) together with one of the tracks above.

A multilingual embedding model then scores every item that passes for similarity to example headlines for each track. Items from curated sources need a score of {{ thresholds.curated }}; keyword matches need {{ thresholds.keyword }}. A language model reads each item that clears the bar. It writes the summary, checks whether the item is really on topic, assigns tracks and a type, and flags possible blog hooks.
